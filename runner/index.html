runner

Each tab or window runs jobs on its own; open more for parallel runs (noisier timings). Keep the worker tab visible and the screen on.

Chrome traces (optional)

To let this device record a Chrome trace of each job (for batches submitted with --trace), install the tracer extension in Chrome, Edge, or ChromeOS:

  1. Download the extension and extract it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extracted profiler-extension folder.

Workers then show "tracing available" in their status line.
